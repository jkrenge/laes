Changelog

What’s new

What changed in lães, newest first. The same list is in the repository.

1.0 (build 9), 27 November 2026

  • On the Mac, one search in the toolbar (⌘F) finds tasks, notes and meetings, typos forgiven, with the current view's results first. It replaces the search fields in Tasks and Notes.
  • On the Mac, Tasks and Notes are calmer: the lists sit on the window's background and the tasks, a task's details and the open note on cards beside them, with no dividers.
  • The toolbar keeps refresh, settings and account together, with hiding private items on its own at the end. Hold ⌘ to see the shortcuts under the buttons. In the weeks, ‹ Today › sits right next to the view switch.
  • Settings › General has Appearance: Light, Dark or Automatic (the default).
  • On the Mac, clicking a to-do's box in a note ticks it again instead of breaking it.

1.0 (build 8), 27 November 2026

  • Search your tasks: one field above the lists finds tasks in every list by title or note, typos too, with done ones below. On the Mac, ⌘F goes to it.
  • A Completed view under Planned, Important and All shows what you finished, by day, from the last 30 days on. Tap or click a tick to reopen a task.

1.0 (build 7), 27 November 2026

  • Notifications have their own pane in Settings, with a card at the top only while the system doesn't allow them. On iPhone and iPad, where the Tasks tab opens is now under General.
  • On the Mac, ⌘1, ⌘2 and ⌘3 switch to Weeks, Tasks and Notes.
  • Tick a to-do by clicking or tapping its box, without editing the note. Done to-dos are greyed out and struck through.
  • The lines of a long list item or to-do start under its text, not under its marker, while reading and while editing.
  • On iPhone and iPad, to-do boxes have the same rounded look, ticked or not.
  • The app icon shows how many to-dos are due today or overdue, and a morning notification (9:00 unless you pick another time) says how many. Both are on from the start; switch them off, or meeting reminders too, in Settings › Notifications.
  • Search forgives typos: “meetnig” finds “meeting”.
  • The notes list shows each note’s first line without its Markdown, and how many of its to-dos are done.
  • The lães settings note sits more quietly at the end of the list.

1.0 (build 4), 27 November 2026

Second beta, and the build sent to App Store review.

  • Send Feedback in Settings, on Mac and iPhone.
  • A prompt when a newer version is out.
  • Crash reports and anonymous usage counts go to the developer, never your calendar, tasks or notes. The privacy policy lists what is sent.

Notes

  • To-dos show as boxes, ticked when done.
  • Outside editing, a note reads clean: the Markdown marks (**, #, link addresses) disappear. They come back, dimmed, while you edit.
  • On the Mac, ⌘S stops editing and saves. Strikethrough moves to ⌘−.
  • On the Mac, the formatting buttons highlight as the pointer passes over them.

iPad and iPhone

  • On iPad, both weeks fit on the screen, and turning the iPad keeps the same days in view.
  • The calendar’s task strip is as tall as the busiest day needs, with tighter rows.
  • On iPad, Tasks shows your lists, the list’s tasks and the task’s details side by side; Notes shows the list beside the open note.
  • Settings moves from the tab bar to a gear next to the eye.

Settings

  • The private group, the order of your lists, the week’s first day and the hours shown are now the same on all your devices. lães keeps them in a read-only note, lães settings, at the end of your notes list. The device that changed them last wins. With notes switched off, each device keeps its own.

1.0, 26 November 2026

First beta for Mac and iPhone.

  • Two weeks on one screen, with tasks above each day’s meetings.
  • Microsoft To Do lists, Planned and Important; drag a task onto a day or into the calendar.
  • Markdown notes in OneDrive, linked to meetings, with a reminder five minutes before.
  • A private group to hide meetings and lists while sharing your screen.
  • Works offline; task changes go out when you’re back online.
  • An MCP server on the Mac for AI tools such as Claude Code.
