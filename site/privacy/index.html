Privacy policy

Your data stays between you and Microsoft

lães shows your Microsoft 365 calendar, tasks and notes. Your calendar, tasks and notes go only to Microsoft, with your own sign-in, and never reach its developer. The app does send crash reports and anonymous usage counts to the developer. This page explains the details. Last updated 26 November 2026.

Who is responsible

lães is made by Julian Krenge as an independent project. For questions about this policy or your data, write to inbox at laesapp.com.

In the app

What lães reads and changes

When you sign in, Microsoft asks you to allow lães to:

  • read your basic profile (your name and email address), to show which account is signed in;
  • read and change your calendar, so it can show meetings and create new ones;
  • read and change your Microsoft To Do tasks;
  • read your Outlook category colours, so meetings and tasks show in the colours you chose;
  • read and write files in a OneDrive folder that only lães uses, for notes. If you turn notes off, lães doesn't ask for this;
  • stay signed in, so you don't have to sign in every time.

lães connects directly from your device to Microsoft. Microsoft's own privacy statement covers what Microsoft does with your data.

What stays on your device

  • A copy of your calendar, tasks and notes, so lães works offline, and changes you made offline that haven't been sent yet.
  • Your sign-in, stored in the Keychain.
  • Your settings, such as the private group and the app icon.

Signing out removes the local copy and the sign-in.

Crash reports, usage counts and feedback

To find and fix problems, lães on the Mac and the iPhone sends the following to a server run by its developer and hosted by Cloudflare:

  • Crash reports: where in the app the failure happened, with your device model, system version and app version.
  • Usage counts: that the app was opened, and that an action such as adding a task or creating a note happened, with your device model, system version, language setting and app version. No titles, text, names, dates or account details.
  • Errors the app didn't expect: the kind of error and its status code, not its message.
  • Feedback you send from Settings, with the app version and device model, and your email address only if you type one in.

Each installation gets a random number that isn't connected to your Microsoft account, your name or your email address, and the server keeps only a scrambled form of it. When the app first starts, the server asks Apple to confirm that the request comes from a genuine Apple device. Your IP address is used to limit how many requests one device can send and isn't stored with the reports; Cloudflare keeps request logs for a few days. There is no advertising identifier.

This is done with three open-source libraries: BoringKit, KSCrash and the Aptabase Swift client. Everything else in lães is Apple's system frameworks.

What lães does not do

  • Your calendar, tasks and notes never reach its developer.
  • It has no advertising, and it doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Reminders and background refresh

Reminders for meeting notes are scheduled on your device. On the iPhone, lães may refresh in the background, again talking only to Microsoft.

AI tools on the Mac

If you connect an AI tool in Settings › AI Access, lães lets that tool read and change your calendar, tasks and notes through a connection on your Mac only (127.0.0.1), protected by a token stored in your Keychain. Items in the private group are hidden from it while the eye is closed. What the tool then does with that data, for example sending it to its provider, is covered by that tool's own privacy policy. The connection is off until you set it up, and you can turn it off at any time.

Beta testing with TestFlight

If you test lães on the iPhone through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares some information with the developer: the email address or name you were invited with, when you installed and used the beta, information about your device, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. This is used only to improve lães. Apple's privacy policy and the TestFlight terms apply.

This website and email

  • laesapp.com is hosted by GitHub Pages. To deliver the pages, GitHub processes your IP address and may keep it briefly in logs for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • The website has no cookies, analytics, trackers or embedded content from other sites.
  • If you write to inbox at laesapp.com, your message and address are kept to answer you and deleted when they're no longer needed, or sooner if you ask. The mailbox is hosted by ALL-INKL.COM in Germany.

Your rights

You can ask what data about you is held, and have it corrected or deleted. Since lães keeps your calendar, tasks and notes only on your devices and with Microsoft, you control those directly: sign out or delete the app, and manage your Microsoft data in your Microsoft account. For emails and TestFlight information, write to inbox at laesapp.com. You can also complain to a data protection authority.

Changes

If lães starts handling data differently, this page changes first, with a new date at the top. The history is public in the repository.
