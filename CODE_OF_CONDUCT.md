# Code of conduct

lães is a small project, and we'd like it to stay a friendly place to ask questions, report problems and suggest ideas.

**Be kind and constructive.** Assume good intent, criticise ideas rather than people, and help newcomers find their way.

**Not welcome:** harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, sharing someone's private information without their permission, and anything else you wouldn't say to a colleague's face.

**If something goes wrong,** use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or contact the maintainer privately through their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and block people who don't follow this code.

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere someone represents the project.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
